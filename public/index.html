Signs PDFs (Adobe Acrobat and similar). Issued by the root above. The key was generated on the YubiKey and can't be exported; the attestation below proves that against Yubico's own CA. PIN and touch required for every signature.

Trust my document signatures in Acrobat

  1. Open a PDF I signed, click the signature, then Signature Properties → Show Signer's Certificate.
  2. Select the top certificate in the chain (my root CA) and check its SHA-1 fingerprint matches listed above.
  3. On the Trust tab, click Add to Trusted Certificates and tick Use this certificate as a trusted root for signed documents. Leave certified documents and dynamic content unticked.

Or download root.crt and import it under Preferences → Signatures → Identities & Trusted Certificates. My root can only issue certificates for me and can't create other CAs, and its key is kept offline.
